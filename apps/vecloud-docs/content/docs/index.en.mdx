## Find the right settings

Open **Domains** and select your website. The sidebar then shows settings for that website, including **Apps**, **Files**, **DNS settings**, **Backups** and **SSL certificates**. Use the domain selector at the top to switch websites.

Available options depend on your account permissions and the services enabled for your website. These guides use the English button labels from the panel so you can recognize them directly.
