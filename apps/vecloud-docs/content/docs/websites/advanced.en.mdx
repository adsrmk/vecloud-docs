Does your application need more capacity or a different hosting setting? Start with what your website actually uses. This helps you decide whether to adjust the application or ask veCloud for help.

## Settings you can manage

Use [Developer tools → PHP](../developers/php.mdx) for available PHP versions, extensions and, if your plan allows it, INI settings. Review [Analytics → Resource usage](../performance/resources.mdx) over a suitable period to understand the load.

## Settings managed by veCloud

veCloud manages PHP-FPM limits, system and disk limits, backup retention and custom [VirtualHost configuration](../developers/vhost.mdx). The internal **Manage overrides** page is not a customer feature. Being the **Owner** of a customer account does not mean you administer the hosting infrastructure.

With suitable account management rights, you can pause new backups under **Backups → Website backups → Disable backups**. This specific [backup setting](../backups/index.mdx) is separate from internal overrides and does not remove existing recovery points.

PHP-FPM handles PHP processes. It differs from an INI setting such as `memory_limit` and from the total memory available to your website environment. Allocating more memory to PHP does not increase that overall limit.

## Discuss an adjustment

Contact support with your domain, the intended behaviour and the problem you want to solve. Include the time and time zone, relevant resource charts and your application’s requirements. Share only relevant log entries without passwords or tokens.

veCloud reviews which adjustment suits your environment. Agree on any impact and a suitable time. After confirmation, test the affected website feature and compare usage again where useful.

## A backup is missing

Follow [backup troubleshooting](../backups/troubleshooting.mdx). Changing backup policy does not resolve a loading error. Ask support to investigate if expected recovery points are missing.
