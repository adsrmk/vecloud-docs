title: Arrange a change of website management
description: Coordinate a website handover with veCloud and keep it separate from team access.

Is another party taking over your website management? Arrange this with veCloud beforehand. Moving a website between customer organisations is an internal administration task, not an action you perform in the customer panel.

## Do you only need to grant access?

With the appropriate account permissions, you can invite a colleague or developer through [Team and access](../account/team.mdx). Select only the websites they need. Website ownership does not need to change for this.

## Discuss the handover

Contact support from your existing account. Include the domain, the intended new managing party and the preferred timing. veCloud coordinates the permissions and details needed from those involved before a transfer can take place.

Discuss who should retain access and who will be responsible for hosting, content and backups. Sharing existing passwords is not a substitute for a handover.

## After confirmation

Confirm with support which account manages the website and whose access should remain. Have outdated team access removed when it is no longer needed.

A management handover does not transfer the domain registration at your registrar. If the registered domain holder must also change, arrange that separately with the registrar.
