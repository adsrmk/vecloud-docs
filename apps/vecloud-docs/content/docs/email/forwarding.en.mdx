Forwarding is useful when you want messages delivered to another existing address. Set the destinations for each address, then test the complete delivery path.

## Manage forwarding addresses

Open **Email → the address → Manage → Forwarders**. Choose **Add forwarder** or **Manage forwarders**. Enter complete destination addresses, one per line, then **Save forwarding addresses**.

Review the whole list before saving. Remove a destination by deleting its line and saving again. An address without a mailbox must retain at least one forwarding destination.

Send a test from another address and check the destination and any local mailbox. Avoid forwarding addresses back to each other; a loop can disrupt delivery.

## A forwarding-only address

Enter the destination under **Forward to**, then choose **Add** to include it in the list. Choose **Forwarding only** during creation and add at least one existing destination. This creates a receiving and forwarding address without its own mailbox storage or sign-in. See [create an email address](./index.mdx).

## Mailbox aliases

Open **Mailbox → Aliases**, enter the additional complete email addresses, one per line, then **Save changes**. Use only addresses whose domain and mail handling you manage. An alias delivers mail for an extra name into this mailbox; it is not a separate mailbox with its own storage and password.

Remove an alias by deleting its line and saving. First check whether forms, contact pages or senders still use it. Use the actual mailbox’s credentials when configuring a mail client.

## Catch-all

Under **Catch-all**, enable receipt for otherwise non-existent addresses on this domain. The switch saves immediately. Check which address should act as catch-all and test with an unused address on the same domain.

Catch-all also collects typing mistakes and substantial spam. Leave it off when you only want explicitly created addresses to receive mail. Turning it off does not delete the regular mailbox or its forwarders.

## A message does not reach its destination

Check the destination spelling and recipient’s spam folder. Also check [MX and local/external routing](../dns-ssl/mail-routing.mdx). Forwarding may affect authentication assessment at an external recipient; a saved forwarder does not guarantee delivery at every provider.
