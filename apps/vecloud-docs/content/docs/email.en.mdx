## Open your email addresses

Open **Email** in the main menu and choose the domain or email address you want to manage.

## Set up a mailbox

Create the email address and enter the requested mailbox details. When adding it to an email client, use the connection details shown in the panel. Copy the server name, port and security setting from the panel.

These details can vary by hosting environment. Your website domain is not automatically the mail server name.

## Forward email

Open the email address and manage its forwarding recipients. Check the destination before saving. A forwarder passes on incoming email; the mailbox settings determine whether you also use the address as a mailbox.

## Spam and block/allow lists

Adjust the available filtering options in the spam settings. Use the block and allow lists for senders you want to block or permit.

## Open webmail

Use the webmail action in the panel to open your mailbox in the browser. Make sure the email DNS records are also correct. Read more in [DNS & SSL](./dns-ssl.mdx).
