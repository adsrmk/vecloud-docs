## View available backups

Open your website and go to **Backups**. The list belongs to the selected website. Check the domain at the top of the panel before continuing.

Each restore point shows which website files and databases are available. The available points depend on the backup settings of your hosting environment.

## Create a backup

Use **Create backup** if this action is available for your website. Wait for the backup to finish before using it as a restore point.

## Restore your website

1. Find the restore point containing the content you want.
2. Choose the restore action for that backup.
3. Select the files and/or databases to restore.
4. Check your selection and confirm the restore.
5. Wait for the restore to finish, then check your website.

A restore replaces the selected current content with content from the backup. Changes made after that restore point may be lost.

## Restore WordPress

A complete WordPress website needs both its files and its associated database. Check both parts when restoring an entire website.
