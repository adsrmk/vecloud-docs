This route takes you from your first sign-in to a website visitors can reach. Work through it in order, using the linked guides whenever you need more detail.

## 1. Sign in to the Control Panel

Open [panel.vecloud.eu](https://panel.vecloud.eu) and sign in with your veCloud account. Go to **Domains** to view your websites.

## 2. Add your domain

Choose **Add domain**, select the domain type and enter the domain name. Use **Addon** for a separate website, **Subdomain** for a subdomain or **Alias** to connect an additional domain to an existing website.

Read more in [Websites & domains](./websites/index.mdx).

<PortalScreenshot name="add-domain" alt="Domain form for creating a website" caption="Choose Addon for a standalone website with its own web space." />

## 3. Check DNS

Open your website and go to **DNS settings**. Check that the domain points to the correct hosting environment. If you manage DNS elsewhere, update the records there.

DNS changes do not become visible everywhere at once. Continue once your domain reaches the correct website.

## 4. Install your website

For WordPress, go to **Apps** and choose **Add application**. Complete the installation, then open **Toolkit** to manage WordPress.

For your own website, upload files through **Files** or your available FTP/SFTP connection. See [Files & databases](./bestanden-databases.mdx).

<PortalScreenshot name="apps" alt="Apps showing WordPress on a demonstration website" caption="Once installed, manage WordPress through Toolkit." />

## 5. Enable HTTPS

Go to **SSL certificates**. If needed, use **Request certificate** to request a certificate for your domain. Check the certificate status, then enable **Enforce HTTPS** if you want visitors to always connect over HTTPS.

<PortalScreenshot name="ssl" alt="SSL table for a demonstration website" caption="Wait until your domain has an active certificate before switching on Enforce HTTPS." />

## 6. View your backups

Open **Backups** and check the available restore points. Read [Backups & restore](./backups/index.mdx) before restoring anything.
