The panel has two levels: your account and your websites. Understanding the difference helps you find the right settings as you move between them.

## Main navigation

Sign in at [panel.vecloud.eu](https://panel.vecloud.eu). **Overview** summarizes your account. **Domains** opens your websites; **Email** opens email management. **Users**, **Integrations** and **Profile** belong to your account or organization.

## Open a website

1. Go to **Domains**.
2. Search for the domain or use the status filter.
3. Click the domain name.
4. Check the selected domain in the topbar before making changes.

The sidebar now shows sections for that website, including **Apps**, **Files**, **Backups** and **DNS settings**. **Domains** in the topbar returns to the list. The domain picker changes the website context when available.

## Search and mobile navigation

Use **Search** in the topbar for global search. On a small screen, open the navigation menu to choose a section. Tables can contain more columns than fit on screen; scroll inside the table to see them.

## A feature is missing

Available actions depend on your role, plan and website. A disabled navigation item is not an active feature. If you cannot see a website, ask its owner to check your access under [Team and access](./team.mdx).

This knowledgebase uses English panel labels. Set your own interface language in [Your profile](./profile.mdx).
