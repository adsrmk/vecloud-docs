Account security starts with your own sign-in. The settings below help you manage your password, set up two-factor authentication and review access to your account.

## Change your password

Open **Profile → Security** and choose **Change** next to your password. Enter the current and new password, then confirm. Use a unique password stored in a password manager.

Cannot sign in? Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the instructions sent to your account address. Do not share a password or recovery message.

## Enable two-factor authentication

Setup has two parts: adding your authenticator, then confirming that its codes work.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the setting

Under **Profile → Security**, choose **Enable** next to two-factor authentication.
</Step>
<Step>
### Connect your authenticator

Scan the QR code using your authenticator app or use the displayed manual key. Do not include that key in a shared screenshot.
</Step>
<Step>
### Confirm with a code

Enter the current six-digit code and confirm. Check that the panel reports two-factor authentication as enabled. Future sign-ins use your password and the code from the app.
</Step>
</Steps>

## A code is rejected

Use a fresh code and check that your phone sets its time automatically. Make sure you selected the correct account in your authenticator. If you lose access to the app, contact support for account assistance; do not share credentials to bypass verification.

## An unfamiliar session

Review **Devices** at the bottom of your profile. Use **Sign out all devices** and change your password if you suspect an unwanted login. Also check the [team members](./team.mdx) who can access your websites.
