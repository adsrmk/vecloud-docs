**Overview** helps you spot which websites need attention. It shows your own account and accessible websites, with links to the relevant management pages.

The sections below appear when available for your account. If a section is missing, check your account and permissions or ask support for help.

## The summary figures

| Figure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Active websites | Active websites in the available overview. |
| Bandwidth usage | Available bandwidth usage for the past 30 days. |
| Pending updates | Known WordPress, plugin and theme updates, rather than a count of websites. |

## Compare bandwidth

**Bandwidth** defaults to **All websites**. This combines accessible websites; an alias is not counted again as a separate website. Choose one site under **Website for statistics** to view its series. The chart uses the last 30 UTC calendar days, including today.

Pay attention to any missing-website-data notice. A partial total is not a complete usage overview. Bandwidth measures data transfer, not unique visitors. For more detail, open [the website’s Analytics](../performance/index.mdx).

## Continue with maintenance

Under **WordPress bulk update**, review updates by category or open the selection with **Update all**. This button does not start updates: you review and confirm the components in the dialog. Read [Bulk WordPress updates](../wordpress/bulk-updates.mdx) for preparation and results.

The **SSL certificates** card checks the public HTTPS connection. Open a status category to see its domains. [The SSL checker](../dns-ssl/certificate-checker.mdx) explains valid, expiring, invalid and unverified results.

## Open a website

Select its domain in **Domains**, or **All domains** for the full list. Then check the domain in the top bar before changing settings. A green general service status does not replace checking your own website.
