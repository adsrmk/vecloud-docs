When colleagues or an external administrator help manage your hosting, give each person their own account and access suited to the websites they work on.

## Who can manage the team?

Open **Users** to view team members. Your own permissions determine whether you can invite or manage others. The organization owner is marked **Owner**.

| Role | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Owns the organization and cannot be removed as an ordinary team member. |
| Superadmin | Broad access to the organization. |
| Collaborator | Access to selected websites. |

These roles apply within your own customer account. **Owner** or **Superadmin** does not grant access to veCloud’s internal infrastructure management. Select only the role and websites the team member needs.

## Invite a team member

Once the required access is clear, prepare the invitation with the appropriate role.

1. Choose **Invite user**.
2. Enter **Name** and **Email**.
3. Select **Invite by email** and/or **Invite with a link**.
4. Select the role. For access to individual websites, choose **Collaborator** and select those websites.
5. Review the selection and click **Invite**.

An invitation link is shown once after creation. Share it only with the intended recipient.

## Change or revoke access

Open an active member’s action menu and choose **Manage** to edit their details and access. Check the selected websites before saving.

For a pending invitation, the menu offers resending by email, creating a new invitation link and revoking the invitation. Remove an active member when they no longer need access. Check the name and consequences in the confirmation.

For personal account security, see [Secure your account](./security.mdx). WordPress users are managed separately in the WordPress toolkit.
