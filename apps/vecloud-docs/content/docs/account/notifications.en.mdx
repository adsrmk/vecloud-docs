## View notifications

Open **Notifications** in the main sidebar. **Current** shows current notifications; **Archive** contains archived notifications. Each row identifies the time, status, description and affected website. Panel announcements appear above the list.

**Critical** calls for immediate investigation, for example when a website is unavailable. **Attention** indicates something to review, such as a certificate issue or an unusual traffic increase. Open the affected website and check the reported cause.

## Unusually high request counts

The panel compares completed hours with the website’s normal traffic. Current detection requires at least 1,000 requests in an hour and a rise to at least five times the normal baseline. Sufficient history is required, including comparable hours on previous days.

A spike can result from a campaign, bot, crawler or abuse. The alert does not establish that an attack is happening and does not automatically block traffic. Review **Analytics**, the timing and recent changes before blocking IP addresses.

## Archive a notification

Use the archive action next to a notification to clear it from your current list. Archiving does not change the website or certificate. Address the underlying cause separately.

## The status in the topbar

The service status in the topbar links to the general VECloud status page. This differs from the availability of an individual website. Investigate a website alert even when the general services are online.

## Insufficient measurements

New websites or missing measurements may not yet support a reliable traffic comparison. No alert is therefore no guarantee of healthy traffic or availability. For a continuing problem, record the domain, time and message for support.
